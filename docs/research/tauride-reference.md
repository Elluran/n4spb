# Tauride Garden: what the real place looks like at the 12 acceptance poses, and where to find public reference photos

This is the reference pack of the Track B acceptance step (docs/TAURIDE-REMAKE.md section 9): per camera pose of `tools/tauride-cams.mjs` a short description of the real place and candidate PUBLIC photo sources.
**Links only: nothing here was downloaded, and no photograph may be committed** (third-party images belong in the git-ignored `tools/tauride/raw/`, and only for a side-by-side comparison). The licence of a
Wikimedia Commons file is stated on its file page: check it before any use beyond looking. The sources were found with web search on 2026-10-22; what a page says is reported as the page says it, what comes from the plan
is marked "plan", and anything not verified is marked so. The user's own photos or a satellite screenshot at the 12 spots remain the acceptance material (the plan, "Requires from the user").

## The place in one paragraph

The Tauride Garden (Таврический сад) was laid out 1783-89 by the gardener William Gould for Prince Potemkin next to the Tauride Palace (Starov); the plan gives 21.1 ha (the OSM garden polygon is 19.8 ha: the palace grounds and strips are outside it).
Gould made two ponds out of the little river Samoroyka, joined by channels, with two islands in the south of the big pond; the hills were heaped from the pond soil (no published heights: the remake's heights are DESIGN values).
The two iron footbridges of 1793-94 (architects F. I. Volkov, K. I. Shpekle; made at the Sestroretsk works) are the first iron-span crossings in Petersburg; the dam between the ponds is a stone overflow dam of the 1790s, a federal monument together with the bridges.
Old oaks, limes and larches stand at the borders, the middle is open lawn; the alleys are pale gravel (pink-beige in photographs; 93 % of the tagged non-bridge ways are compacted, earth or ground in the OSM surface tags); a cast-iron fence of 190 cm surrounds the garden
(Wikipedia gives 1869 for the cast-iron fence replacing a log fence, the plan says 1896: not verified here). Sources: the plan (section 5, "Reality"), docs/research/north-tauride.md, the pages below.

## Sources used for the descriptions (read, not copied)

* [citywalls.ru, "Таврический сад. Мосты и плотина"](https://www.citywalls.ru/house31112.html): bridges No.3 "Big Bridge" (1793-94, five iron arches on granite-clad abutments, span 13.15 m, "connects the Big island with the eastern part of the park") and No.4 "Small Bridge" (four iron arches, span 10.6 m, about 3 m wide, "connects the Big island with the south-western part of the park"); No.1 wooden arched (2000s), No.2 a metal bridge in the 18th-century style (2000s), No.5 first on 1966 imagery; the dam, a stone overflow dam of the 1790s between the two ponds, federal monument. Photo captions: bridge No.3 from the south, south-west, south-east, north-west, north, its supports, deck and railing; bridge No.4 (4 views); the dam from the south and the west (2019). ## Per pose

**T01 South gate (1226, -1278), looking at the gate n828488069 from 10 m inside.** Stone gate piers with iron leaves in the cast-iron fence, the street and the Tavricheskaya houses beyond; behind the camera the main alley, pale gravel, limes either side. Gates of 1822 (plan). Source: Category:Tauride Garden (look for "gate", "ворота", "вход").

**T02 Iron bridge No.3 (1404.5, -1438), looking at the chain (1394, -1461).** The Big Bridge: five iron arches on granite-clad abutments, cast-iron plate deck, railings of interlocking ovals and circles, span 13.15 m; it joins the Big island to the east bank. Seen from the south-east across the arm, lake behind. Sources: Category:Bridges in Tauride garden; the citywalls.ru captions "Мост №3 Вид с юго-востока / с юга"; the public-domain "Bridge lake obelisk" file.

**T03 Pioneers-heroes memorial (1384, -1439), looking at (1371, -1452).** A limestone monument 4.8 m high (1962) on the high point at the north tip of the island, with a limestone descent to the water; the lake and, far behind it, the palace to the north. Source: the Commons category of the monument (11 files).

**T04 Wooden bridge No.1 (1113.5, -1605.5), looking at (1103, -1586).** A wooden arched (humped) footbridge with railings over the south bay of the west pond, 15 m; the pond edge and its planting. Source: Category:Bridges in Tauride garden (look for the timber deck); citywalls.ru "Мост №1" if present.

**T05 Dam (1265, -1653.5), looking at (1272, -1674).** The stone overflow dam of the 1790s between the Small and the Big pond with the footbridge on it, the channel running away north-west. The position is the OSM bridge way w112880645 (docs/TAURIDE-REMAKE.md section 6.2). Sources: citywalls.ru "Плотина Вид с юга / с запада" (2019); Category:Ponds of the Tauride Garden.

**T06 Iron bridge No.4 (1330.5, -1405), looking at (1333.5, -1427).** The Small Bridge: four iron arches, span 10.6 m, about 3 m wide, railings of stylised brushes; it joins the Big island with the south-west of the park. Seen from the south bank. Sources: citywalls.ru "Мост №4" (4 views); mostotrest-spb.ru "4-й в Таврическом саду мост"; Category:Bridges in Tauride garden.

**T07 Football pitch (1353, -1356.5), looking at (1383, -1356).** The marked pitch (3,917 m2) in the south-east, behind its fence with several openings, goals, the surrounding alleys and trees; the sports centre building w30198530 to the east. Sources: Category:Tauride Garden (sports grounds); any recent street-level or aerial photograph.

**T08 Fountain "Boy with a duck" (1279.5, -1392), looking at (1282.6, -1400.5).** The bronze boy with the duck (1910, plan) on a basin, 9 m away, alley and trees round it. Source: Category:Tauride Garden (not in the Favourite fountain category); tripadvisor reviews mention it but their photographs are not free.

**T09 West gate (934, -1412), looking at the gate n950150459 from 10 m inside.** The west gate in the fence, the alley leading to it; the mapped service lane (w1258039516 / 17) enters from the same gate. Source: Category:Tauride Garden.

**T10 Open meadow (1100, -1350), looking north at the tree belt (1104, -1440).** Mown lawn in the foreground, a belt of old oaks, limes and ash on the far side, no building in view; this is the "open lawns in the middle" of the plan. Sources: Category:Tauride Garden; the 2016 aerial for the lawn / canopy pattern.

**T11 Palace garden facade (1456.5, -1578.5), looking at (1379.9, -1643).** The south (garden) front of the palace across the lake: a long, low yellow building with a high central block and a shallow green dome, white cornices and pilasters, the lake and its island in front; about 100 m. The north (street) front with the six-column Doric portico is the VIEWS pose Digit6. Sources: Category:Tauride Palace; the 2016 aerial file; the palace's official history page.

**T12 Aerial (1200, -1250, 230 m, pitch -65, north).** An oblique overview of the southern half of the garden: the alleys, the lake, the arms round the island, the pitch; pale-gravel alleys against dark canopy. Sources: the 2016 aerial file (CC BY-SA 4.0), the Wikimapia aerial; the user's satellite screenshot for the numeric overlay (`tools/tauride-overlay.mjs`).
