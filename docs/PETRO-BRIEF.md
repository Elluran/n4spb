## Rules that bite here
* **No flags** (the Flagstaff tower of the fortress, the Aurora, the Nakhimov school, the Exchange, the Mosque normally carry flags / ensigns / pennants: omit all of them, no flagpoles; a bare mast of a ship is fine), no sirens / alarms / gun shots (the noon gun is a silent prop), summer day, anime cel-shaded, realistic. Heights: street y = 0; the Neva water is -3.4, small canals -2.7 ... -3.4.
* **The player walks where a tourist walks**: fortress courtyards, the wall walk where it is public, the beach, the Spit descent to the water, the park alleys, the piers, the deck of nothing that is closed (the Aurora and interiors stay closed: solid). Gates are passable. Nobody walks on the water: every bank you own needs a collider or a quay (test with a walk probe: copy a `tools/landmarks*-walk.mjs`).
* **Facade labels** reserve through `ctx.services.labels.reserve(...)` (`docs/rules/LABELS-API.md`); plaques with the real Russian text of your sight are welcome. **Walls**: no cracks to neighbours (`docs/rules/FACADE-INTEGRITY.md`, `npm run walls`). **Road paint / traffic signs / plates** are not yours.
* **Road integrity**: nothing on a carriageway, paving clipped at `net.cover`; audit your area with `node tools/roads-furniture-audit.mjs --strict --near x,z,300` and `node tools/roads-surface-audit.mjs --near x,z,300` (the full `npm run roads` takes 4 minutes and 8 GB: the lead runs it).
* Statues from the shared figure kit (`src/world/lib/figures.js`); the user judges monuments at 1-3 m: no boxes. Everything repeated more than ~40 times instanced. Budgets: <= ~300 k triangles per landmark module (the fortress may use ~450 k), build <= 1.5 s. The scene is already heavy (17 M triangles): prefer the facade shader and instancing over modelled repetition.
* Z-fighting is a bug class (`node tools/zfight.mjs`, target < 0.02 %). Shots: `node tools/shot.mjs --only environment,streets,city,<module> --cams "..." --out shots/<module>/a --t 20` (4 numbers = walking eye x,z,yaw,pitch; 5 = free x,y,z,yaw,pitch; yaw 0 north, 90 west, 180 south, -90 east); ONE screenshot job at a time (shared GPU, many agents: if a job is slow, wait, do not start a second one); LOOK at every PNG against the reference photos; include a close-up (1-3 m), tourist street level, an aerial and a long vista (the fortress spire and the Spit are seen from the Palace Embankment and the Trinity Bridge 1 km away: check those views).
* **Another session works in this checkout too.** Never run `git add` / `commit` / `stash` / `checkout` / `reset` / `clean`, never `npm run bake` (the lead commits and bakes). Never delete or rewrite files you do not own. Russian text only through Write / Edit (never shell heredocs). Do not touch `../100_3d_moscow_city`, `../101_3d_yakutsk`, `../sakuragaoka-station`. Keep your module loadable at all times (small steps, `node tools/check.mjs <module> city environment streets` often; risky rewrites beside the old file, then swap).
