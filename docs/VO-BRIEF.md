## What the island looks like (every builder reads this)
* **A rotated Petrine grid.** The numbered Lines run at bearing 150 / 330 (NNW-SSE, Neva to Malaya Neva), Bolshoy, Sredny and Maly prospects at 60 / 240 (ENE-WSW, towards Gavan); nothing is axis-aligned. Lines come in PAIRS that share one street ('4-5-я линии', one carriageway, odd side nearer the Spit), pairs 155-167 m apart, prospects 490-505 m apart. Helper: u along the prospects, v along the Lines, x = -3382 - 0.866u - 0.5v, z = -1009 + 0.5u - 0.866v (origin = Bolshoy x Kadetskaya line).
* **Three different prospects**: Bolshoy is an 85 m canyon (19 m central transit roadway + two lime boulevards + two side lanes, trolleybuses, no tram); Sredny 31 m with the only tram line in a 6 m median; Maly 40 m, trolleybuses. The Lines are 29 m facade to facade. The embankments (Universitetskaya, Lt Schmidt, Makarova) are granite: wall ~3 m + parapet ~1 m, black cast-iron lamp standards, descents with stairs.
* **Low and old in the east, newer to the west**: median 4 storeys. Neva front and the first 7 Lines: Petrine baroque and classicism in ochre / yellow / salmon with white orders and grey-green roofs (Menshikov Palace chrome yellow, Academy peach and orange-pink, Cadet Corps salmon); eclectic tenements in the middle; Northern Modern (Art Nouveau) from the 8th Line westwards; Stalinist blocks in Gavan; 1950s brick and 1965-90 panel superblocks; 14-25 storey blocks on the 2006-26 land fill (open superblocks, no courtyard wells, firewalls or arches there).
* **Water**: the Bolshaya Neva turns SSW at x about -4150 and reaches the Bay near (-5000,1600) (the river / sea boundary is the Blagoveshchensky Bridge); the Malaya Neva continues as the Smolenka, a 3.7 km, 20-40 m wide canal-like river that was dug straight into the Gulf in the 1960-70s; Galerny Harbour and the Shkipersky canal are Petrine; the Gulf shore moved 2-3 km west with the Marine Facade fill (no beach). Street y = 0, Neva -3.4.
* **Big empty tracts**: the Smolensky cemetery (51.7 ha, no trees or graves in the bake), the industrial south-west shore (tanneries, CHP-7, Sevkabel, Baltic Shipyard with portal cranes, no continuous public embankment), the wind-swept sand lots of the land fill.
* **Not on the island**: Dekabristov Island (the Lutheran and Armenian cemeteries, the siege cemetery, Primorskaya metro, Morskoy Kaskad) lies across the Smolenka although part of it is inside the frame; Krestovsky (Gazprom Arena) is far north.

## Rules that bite here
* **No flags, no flagpoles** (the Naval Corps turret, the Mining University, the Krasin and S-189 sterns, the Sea Terminal foot, the Sevkabel sea square, the kronshpits spires, Pribaltiyskaya's 3-4 poles, the port, the Baltic Shipyard, the Blagoveshchensky Bridge normally carry them; 49 flagpoles are mapped on the island: skip all). Bare spires and masts are fine. **No sirens / alarms / horns**: the fire stations (Bolshoy 73 with the brick watch tower, the Gavan fire reserve) stand silent without beacons; every bascule stays DOWN with no warning bells; the Tesla coil, the guns of the D-2 and the Kirov turret are silent static props; no ship horns. Summer day, anime cel-shaded, realistic, no corporate logos (plain wordmarks such as the roof letters МОРСКОЙ ВОКЗАЛ and ПРИБАЛТИЙСКАЯ are fine).
* **The grid is rotated**: never axis-align a block; use the u, v helper and the baked footprints.
* **The baked heights of the sights are WRONG** (levels x 3.3, many null): Academy 9.9 m (real cornice ~21 m), Smolensk church 9.9, Xenia chapel 3.3, Mining University 6.6, Sea Terminal null, Pribaltiyskaya 6.6: your claim replaces them. Claiming a record of an OLD part file (Academy r165182 in S4, Naval Corps r1242815 in S3, Rumyantsev garden w638336438 in S4) is allowed (they are in the scenery margin of the old regions); the Mining University is baked as `r2902646`, not the OSM way the research names. OSM relations are cut by tile edges: St Michael r18085303 and the Dormition r17488566 give wrong centroids, use the way ids of the dossier.
* **The player walks where a tourist walks**: sphinx pier steps, the Academy forecourt, Rumyantsev and Academic gardens, Andreevsky boulevard, the cemetery paths (open gates), Sevkabel promenade, the Sea Terminal forecourt, Gavan courtyards that are public. Closed territory is exterior only and solid: the Naval Corps yards, the Deryabin barracks, the shipyard, the Miluyushchaya church (inside the Submarine Training Detachment), CHP-7. Nobody walks on the water: every bank you own needs a collider or a quay (probe it: copy a `tools/landmarks*-walk.mjs`).
* **Road integrity** (AGENT-BRIEF): nothing on a carriageway, every road asphalt, paving clipped at `net.cover`. Island specifics: two carriageways of one street closer than 7.5 m are ONE roadway (Maly pr. east 6-9 m, 8-9 / 14-15 / 16-17 lines 8-11 m); the Bolshoy boulevards are real (own pavement clip); Sredny has a tram median: nothing within 1.2 m of a rail; ulitsa Repina is 5.6 m with setts: no poles; Andreevsky boulevard is a pedestrian zone (agree its surface with streets). Audit your area: `node tools/roads-furniture-audit.mjs --strict --near x,z,300` and `node tools/roads-surface-audit.mjs --near x,z,300` (the full `npm run roads` is the lead's).
* **Facade labels / plates / paint** are not yours: reserve through `ctx.services.labels.reserve(...)`; plates carry the real Russian text of your sight; walls without holes (`docs/rules/FACADE-INTEGRITY.md`, `npm run walls`).
* **Respect**: the cemeteries and memorials (Smolensky, Kirov memorial, siege memorials) are calm places: generic grave archetypes (no invented names; only documented plaque texts), no skulls, no horror; the Cherkassky house is a ruin behind a hoarding; the Lenexpo site is a building site.
* **Cemetery / repeated detail**: everything repeated more than ~40 times is instanced (graves, trees, columns, balusters, cranes); statues come from `src/world/lib/figures.js`; the user judges monuments at 1-3 m: no boxes. Use the facade shader for window grids.
* **Budgets**: <= ~300 k triangles per module (cemetery 400 k, Academy 320 k), build <= 1.5 s. The scene grew to 52 km2 (environment 22 s, streets 42 s, city 52 s to build; 4.9 M streets triangles): prefer the facade shader and instancing over modelled repetition; keep the silhouette right from 1-3 km (the Academy dome, the cathedral spires, the Pribaltiyskaya slab, the Sea Terminal spire, the cranes are skyline).
* **Data traps**: the bake is region-locked (never re-bake: the lead does, with the lock); `assets/baked` is stale for the island, so shots build from source (never `?baked=force`); the cemetery interior is EMPTY in the data (you plant it); highway=construction / proposed roads are dropped by the bake; building:colour on the island is noise.
* **Z-fighting is a bug class** (`node tools/zfight.mjs`, target < 0.02 %).
