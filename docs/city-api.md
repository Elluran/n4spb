# City module API (`src/world/city.js`, `src/world/city/**`)

> **Strip cut.** The API below is unchanged, but the district grammar of the Petrograd side, Vasilievsky Island, Krestovsky and the islands (`district.js` zones, `VO_MIX`, `known-data.js` blocks VO / Petro / Mars / SW, the `tools/city-known-*` and `tools/city-petro-audit.mjs` generators) was removed together with the regions it served: the strip keeps only the OLD / W1 / N1 / W2a..e records (see [STRIP.md](STRIP.md)). The sections about those districts are history; `docs/research/` is not part of this repository.

Owner: the CITY specialist. Draws every OSM building / part that no landmark module owns (`lib/claims.js`; claimed but unowned buildings are drawn in the normal grammar with a terracotta
blockout colour; owned ones are skipped but still act as occluders for the neighbours' party walls). Heights are `L.heightOf(b)` (cornice height); mansards / roofs are added on top.

## Pipeline
`prep.prepare` (draw records: raised parts get their real thickness, bases synthesised under raised parts, tiny roof lifts capped) -> `archetypes.describe` (KNOWN houses from `known.js`, neighbour heights from `heights.js`) -> `edges.analyse` -> `edges.analyse` (street-wall graph) -> `passages.planPassages` (arches) -> `walls.buildWalls` (shader quads) + `cornice.addCornice` + `roofs.addDeck` -> details (`details.js`) -> `meshes.CityMeshes.flush`.
Everything is collected into streams per 160 m cell (walls / core / fine / roof / rails), one noBatch mesh per stream and cell (draw calls, not triangles, were the cost). `fine` (pipes, chimneys, dormers, AC, awnings, scaffold) is hidden beyond ~260 m by `instances.LOD`.

## Per-house features of KNOWN entries
`a` archetype, `f` storeys, `H` cornice (m), `hex` wall, `tr` trim class (0 default warm white, 1 white, 2 wall colour, 3 cream, 4 light grey stone, 5 dark stone, 6 green-grey patina: flag bits 16-18 of the wall attribute, `wallmat.trimFlags`), `rf` roof colour, `ms` mansard (inset retried at 1.2 / 0.8 m on complex footprints), `er` erkers (only on eclectic / tenement / nouveau), `tu` corner turret (unknown houses keep the hash), `granite` (flag bit 19: rough-hewn 1.1 x 0.55 m blocks over the whole street wall), `shop` true / false, `ga` carriage gate [fraction along the longest street front, width] (a vault is built only when a visible wall of the same house lies straight behind it), `custom` ('duma').

Petrograd-side fields (block PETRO of `known-data.js`, generated by `node tools/city-known-petro.mjs` from the houses tables of the sight dossiers + `tools/city-known-petro-curated.mjs`): `gb` gables per street front (0-2), `gs` gable shape (0 round, 1 shouldered, 2 steep, 3 stepped), `mj` majolica band, `so` rough granite socle that steps up, `pp` roof parapet `'bal'` / `'attic'` / 0, `col` half-columns, `tt` turret top (`helm` / `tent` / `spire` / `dome`), `y` year (information), `c` confidence. An entry WITHOUT `hex` takes its colour from the district palette at run time (a dossier `wallGuess` is one colour per style class: it would paint a street in three colours). `rf` colours the deck and the turret top of district houses.

## Petrograd-side archetypes and the district-aware default (2026-10, `district.js`, `gables.js`)
The Petrograd side (regions P1..P5) is the Art Nouveau district: Northern Modern and the neoclassical revival of 1899-1916, then constructivism / Stalinist fronts on the avenues and brick industry at the edges; the Spit and the first lines of Vasilievsky Island (V1, V2 and the south bank of the Malaya Neva inside P2) are low yellow-and-white classicism. The grammar of the older regions is FROZEN: nothing below applies to a record of the 23 part files that existed before this extension (`district.OLD_REGIONS`: OLD, W1, N1, W2a..W2e, E1, N2, N3, M1..M7, S1..S5; `district.regionOf(b)` is null for them), and `node tools/city-signature.mjs` proves it (see Regression signature). EVERY OTHER record is new (`district.NEW_REGIONS` = the rest of `data/city.js REGIONS`: P1..P9, V1, V2 today): a rectangle added to the frame later gets the district rules with no edit of the module. The district itself is geometry, not a region name: `districtOf` cuts off the Vyborg bank of the Bolshaya Nevka and the south bank of the Neva (generic mix, but still "new" for canopies, ships, heights and the data rules below).

## Vasilievsky Island grammar (2026-10-09, `district.js` voZoneOf / VO_MIX, `known-data.js` block VO)
It applies only to the records of the island rectangles V3..V12 (`district.isVoFresh(b)`: the id, the parent or the base of a part is in `city-v3..v12.js`): `st.fresh`. V1, V2 (the Spit, Makarova row) and the Petrograd side keep the grammar of the Petro round bit for bit. Source of every number: `docs/research/vo-roster-architecture.md` sections 4-6.

|---|---|---|

Turrets on BEVELS (`erkers.js turretCorners`): two edges of >= 7 m with a chain of short edges between them (a chamfer or an arc, bending 55-126 degrees in all, every bend convex, at most 14 m long) are one corner; the virtual vertex is the outermost point of the bevel on the bisector, so the tower stands on the chamfer. Used in pass 1 for houses the research flags (`tu`, any region; tried after the simple corners, junction corners first, a pedestrian street counts as a street front) and in pass 0 for island records. The older regions keep their hash-chosen turrets bit for bit. Never two towers within 5 m of each other on one house. Second pass (2026-10-09): in pass 1 the FIRST tower of a flagged house accepts a ground piece of 2.2 m instead of 3 m on one of its two walls (`gmin` in `erkers.js`: Dekabristov 36/1 has 2.6 m of its end wall free beside a taller neighbour; Theatre Sq 8 gets its junction corner first); a second tower keeps 3 m. Result: 35 of the 55 research-flagged families carry a tower (was 34; `tools/city-tower-audit.mjs --strict` 3785 features, 0 defects). The 20 that cannot: 5 island corner houses whose corner vertex is shared with a neighbouring footprint that overlaps the tower circle (Kadetskaya 23 / Tuchkov, 3rd Line 20, 7th Line 26, 4th Line 5 / Bolshoy 1, 12th Line 35: OSM data, the crowding test refuses them), 5 where one wall of every candidate corner is a party wall with no ground piece (Nevsky 64, 5th Sovetskaya 7-9, 13th Line 16, 9th Line 30, Maly 7), 7 that are mid-block houses (no corner facing two streets: Nevsky 32A, Bolshoy P.S. 17, 7th Line 32, 12th Line 31 / 33, 14th Line 65, 15th Line 46) and 3 with fewer than 4 storeys (Kadetskaya 27, Bolshoy 73, Bolshoy 85).

Solid labels (`slots.js`): awnings, door canopies with their steps, downpipes and AC units also reserve their world box and are refused when their ORIENTED footprints overlap by more than 1 cm along each edge normal and in height (the audit's own separating-axes test; the review replaced the first axis-aligned test, which refused 1 000 AC units and 243 awnings for 7 real pairs): oblique corners, jogs of the street line and pipe / AC pairs across a corner no longer cross (`npm run labels`: 7 pairs -> 0). Door visors are a 6 cm sheet on two tie rods and wall brackets (no black floating slab); steps and visors are not built over a carriageway or a service way (`net.cover`).

Wall audit rules added: intervals of a wall narrower than 5 cm are kept down to 5 mm in the island rectangles V3..V12 only (`isVoFresh`; a neighbour's corner within 12 cm cut a 2 cm slit: w539286853), and a raised `roof` part of a house (min_height 3.3 m mansard roofs: w537736642 / 644 / 646) is a roof on posts up to its mapped top (island only too: the reviewed V1 / V2 / Petrograd records keep their output). A mapped OSM plaque within 6 m of a door suppresses the statistical plaque field there (props draws the real one).

Frozen-district exceptions of the island round (`city-signature --ref 04e4731` against the working tree, reviewed 2026-10-10): OLD 18 + S3 2 records = KNOWN rows of the Oktyabrsky and Naval Corps tables (requested by the sight modules); V1 / V2 / P2: KNOWN rows of the Makarova / Spit tables and the colour assignment of their neighbours; 227 `city-fine` cells of the old city = the visor, the steps off the carriageway and the label-collision fixes (global defect fixes); every other old cell identical.

## Regression signature (`tools/city-signature.mjs`)
`--out file.json` builds the city alone and hashes (1) every record: style + wall records, grouped by data region, (2) every mesh: vertex buffers per stream and 320 m cell (sign boards by position: their atlas uv depends on every sign made before). `--ref HEAD` builds the module as committed at a git ref (read-only `git show` into `shots/city/_ref-<pid>`, `tools/city-ref.mjs`) over the CURRENT data and claims, so the baseline does not drift while the landmark agents change their claims. `--diff a.json b.json` fails when a record of a frozen region or a mesh of a cell no new-region house touches differs. The new regions are not listed in the tool: each signature stores `district.NEW_REGIONS` of the module it was built from and the diff takes the union of both sides (`--new a,b` overrides). `[--ref HEAD] --dump id,id` prints the raw style + wall records of these ids: run it with and without `--ref` and diff the two outputs to see WHY a record changed. Records that a landmark owns are in the signature too (the city is built alone, nothing is claimed): the 104 parts of the Exchange in V1 change there with any rule about raised parts although the scene never draws them from this module. `tools/city-wall-audit.mjs --ref HEAD` answers the same question for a wall-audit failure (new, or there before?).

## Services
`ctx.services.city = { buildings, triangles, entrances: [{x,z,rotY,bldg}] (rotY faces outward: parade doors + shop doors), walls (wall records), styles: Map(id -> style), report, buildMs }`.

## Facade integrity (docs/rules/FACADE-INTEGRITY.md)
Every wall edge has a face or an allow-listed reason (`node tools/city-wall-audit.mjs`), every unit / door offset `u.s`, `u.door`, `d.s` is LOCAL to `w.a` (never subtract `w.s0`), every facade label reserves a slot (`node tools/city-label-audit.mjs`).

## Rules followed
Cyrillic text only from POIS / generic lists (chain brands and Latin names replaced by invented generic names); no logos; no flags or flagpoles; albedo <= ~0.92; windows / signs recessed or offset >= 2 cm, signs use polygonOffset; party walls
never drawn twice (zfight sweep: < 0.01 % noisy pixels); colliders are thin boxes on visible pieces with gaps at arches.
